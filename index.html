About us

Gyan Automation supplies genuine industrial automation products: PLC, HMI, VFD, servo drives, SMPS, sensors, contactors and relays from trusted brands. Every order ships with a GST invoice.

Privacy policy

We collect your name, mobile, email and address only to process and deliver orders. We do not sell your data. Payments are handled by Razorpay; we never see or store card details. You can ask us to delete your data by contacting us.

Terms of sale

  • All prices are in INR and include GST unless stated.
  • Orders are confirmed after stock check; we may cancel and refund if an item is unavailable.
  • Delivery time is an estimate and may vary by courier and location.
  • Product images are for reference; specifications follow the manufacturer datasheet.

Refund and return policy

  • Report damaged or wrong items within 48 hours of delivery with photos.
  • Items must be unused, in original packing with all accessories.
  • Programmed, opened or used electronic items are not returnable, except for manufacturing defects covered by the brand warranty.
  • Approved refunds go to the original payment method within 5 to 7 working days. COD refunds are made by bank transfer.
